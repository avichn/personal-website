Moment 01

First Date

My cutu,

It's been two years since we went on that date, the perfect date. I did not want to write a letter because I usually don't and it would sort of be like I'm only writing it on an occasion. But this is really special to me. I still remember how nervous I was the day before, how I could not sleep the whole night. I remember practising what I would say to you when I saw you and hoping you would find me attractive. All of that went out the door the moment I saw you. You tore my walls down with your beautiful sparkly eyes and suddenly I was a kid again, talking about anything that came to my mind. It felt like meeting a best friend after years and it felt like I had finally met my soulmate, as cringe as it sounds. Since then every moment of my life has been worth it. If I'm making you laugh or when I'm teasing you, even when I make you angry. You gave meaning to my life without even knowing or trying, just by being there. It's the first time I've felt so responsible, because I've never had anyone this precious. I have tears in my eyes as I write this. I know you didn't want to talk about it much today since I don't usually talk about the first date but since it is that day, it brings back so many feelings. I don't know where I would be without you baby. I'm so grateful for the joy you've brought in my life. My whole life really, every little aspect. I have no idea how you made me feel safe since the second I saw you. I've gotten that feeling every day, every moment since and I know I always will. And that is why I will keep you safe, loved and secure till the day I die.

It's scary to even think about what would have happened if I didn't meet you 2 years ago today. I'm so grateful.

I love you so much cutu, you're my heart, my life, my everything.

Forever yours
Fake Jaat
